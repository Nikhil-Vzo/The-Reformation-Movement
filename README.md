The official landing page and advocacy portal for **The Reformation Movement**, an initiative led by Samuel Palaechi dedicated to youth empowerment, civic awareness, socio-economic reform, and leadership development across Nigeria.

---

## 📌 Mission & Pillars

The Reformation Movement is dedicated to building sustainable, youth-led civic solutions across four core pillars:
1. **Youth Leadership**: Cultivating grassroots ethical leadership and governance skills.
2. **Civic Education**: Educating citizens on constitutional rights, electoral transparency, and community organization.
3. **Economic Empowerment**: Mentorship programs and vocational skills training for young professionals.
4. **Community Action**: Direct local mobilization and social impact outreach initiatives.

---

## ✨ Web Platform Features

- **Manifesto & Vision Canvas**: High-impact editorial storytelling outlining the movement's declaration and charter.
- **Strategic Action Pillars**: Interactive grid illustrating active initiatives and regional chapters.
- **Member Onboarding ("Join Us")**: Streamlined participation form connecting volunteers and advocates directly with organizers.
- **Optimized Visual Branding**: High-fidelity vector assets and national identity styling.
